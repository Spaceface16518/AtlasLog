# Data acknowledgements

- Offline city data includes a global sample and 12 Washington, DC-area cities from GeoNames `cities15000`, downloaded October 26, 2026. GeoNames data is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). Washington's display name, local aliases and state labels are adapted for the app.
- Geographic resource design targets Natural Earth 1:110m public-domain data: naturalearthdata.com.
- Continent and subcontinent membership follows the United Nations Statistics Division M49 geographic regions: unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49.
- The four U.S. large regions and nine U.S. travel divisions use the [U.S. Census Bureau 2025 Cartographic Boundary Files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2025/geo/carto-boundary-file.html), at 1:5,000,000. Census geographic data is public domain. These are statistical groupings, not cultural borders; Washington, DC is in South Atlantic within the South.
- Washington Capital Region follows the [National Capital Planning Commission's National Capital Region definition](https://www.ncpc.gov/docs/publications/Resource_Guides_Full_2024.pdf). Its boundary combines 2025 Census county equivalents at 1:500,000: DC; Montgomery and Prince George's in Maryland; Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William in Virginia; and Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, Manassas and Manassas Park independent cities. It excludes Baltimore, Annapolis and Frederick. Boundaries are simplified for maps, not surveying or exact border decisions.
- Apple Maps supplies network map imagery, search suggestions, selected-place resolution, and reverse geocoding.

The bundle contains 42 cities, complete country-composed continent and
subcontinent geometry for the bundled Natural Earth countries, nationwide U.S.
region/division coverage (50 states and DC), Washington Capital Region, and
simplified representative cultural travel regions elsewhere. U.S. territories
are outside the Census region/division system.
