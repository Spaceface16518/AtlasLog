# Data acknowledgements

- Major-city sample fields follow the GeoNames `cities15000` format. GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: geonames.org.
- Geographic resource design targets Natural Earth 1:110m public-domain data: naturalearthdata.com.
- Apple Maps supplies network map imagery, search suggestions, selected-place resolution, and reverse geocoding.

This development build includes a small, globally distributed city fixture and simplified representative region geometry. It does not claim complete geographic coverage.
