# Data acknowledgements

- Major-city sample fields follow the GeoNames `cities15000` format. GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: geonames.org.
- Geographic resource design targets Natural Earth 1:110m public-domain data: naturalearthdata.com.
- Continent and subcontinent membership follows the United Nations Statistics Division M49 geographic regions: unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49.
- Apple Maps supplies network map imagery, search suggestions, selected-place resolution, and reverse geocoding.

This development build includes a small, globally distributed city fixture,
complete country-composed continent and subcontinent geometry for the bundled
Natural Earth countries, and simplified representative travel-region geometry.
